Product description

Custom 5.7inch 320×240 Monochrome LCD Module Available in yellow-green background with black text

Description:

The JM320240A-GSBLWFA5 is a 320×240 monochrome LCD module designed for reliable performance in diverse applications.

Featuring an STN (Blue) transmissive/negative LCD type and additionally supporting FSTN positive-transflective display, it offers flexible visibility options to suit different environmental needs. The module supports 8080/6800 series MCU interfaces and is driven by the RA8835 controller, ensuring compatibility with standard microcontroller systems.

1. Product Specifications and Features

Display Size: 320 x 240 dots with a dot size of 0.33x0.33mm and pitch of 0.36x0.36mm.

Mechanical Dimensions: 160.00(L)×109.00(W)×11.00(H) mm, with a viewing area of 122.00×92.00 mm.

Electrical Parameters: Operates at VDD=5.0V (system power) and VOP=24.0V (LCD driver), with an -20°C to 70°C operating temperature range and -30°C to 80°C storage temperature range.

Backlight: White side-mounted LED backlight (VDD=5.0V, IDD=180mA), delivering 90-220 Cd/m² luminance.

Color Options: Available in yellow-green background with black text, blue background with white text, and gray background with black text to suit different design needs.

Rugged Design: Built to withstand industrial environments with stable performance across temperature ranges.

What this specification means in practice

TN glass is the cost-efficient choice with the fastest response. Contrast and colour shift once you move off the specified viewing direction, so it fits devices with a fixed operator position.

Operating range -20°C to +70°C covers unheated cabinets, outdoor enclosures and cold-chain equipment. Standard commercial panels stop at 0 °C and go dark or slow in the cold.

How a customised order runs

  1. Send the requirement

    Size, resolution, interface, brightness, operating temperature, touch or no touch, and the annual quantity. A photo or drawing of the enclosure opening helps more than a long description.

  2. We confirm feasibility and quote

    You get a specific model number or a modification plan, with tooling cost and lead time stated separately from the unit price. Usually within one working day.

  3. Sample and approval

    Samples in 10-15 days from stock glass; custom glass or a new FPC adds tooling time. You approve dimensions, display quality and interface timing before mass production starts.

  4. Production and shipment

    10-20 days after specification freeze. Anti-static bag + foam tray + export carton, shipped DHL / FedEx / UPS express or by your forwarder. We keep the approved sample as the reference for every repeat order.
