Our LCD module product line offers a wide range of resolutions, from 122×32 to 320×240, with standardized size options to meet diverse application needs.

Key resolutions include: 122×32, 160×32, 128×32, 128×64, 160×160, 160×96, 192×64, 160×128, 240×64, 240×128, and 320×240.

2. Core Specifications

2.1 Resolution Flexibility

We provide multiple resolution configurations to address varying performance requirements.

2.2 Interface Diversity

Supports major industry interfaces, including parallel and serial ports, ensuring seamless integration with various host systems.

2.3 Customizable Backlight & LCD Attributes

Backlight brightness, color, and LCD color can be customized. High-brightness options are optimized for sunlight-readable performance in outdoor environments.

Customization of LCD size, structure, display color, interface type, FPC layout design, and appearance for tailored mechanical or branding needs.

3. Product technical parameters

Our standard technical parameters include customizable resolution, LCD size, control driver and surface luminance; STN/FSTN display type; parallel or serial interface; 3.3~5V driving voltage; and yellow, white, blue, orange or red backlight options. Operating temperature is 20°C to +70°C and storage temperature is -30°C to +80°C. The modules are designed for REACH and RoHS compliance.

What this specification means in practice

TN glass is the cost-efficient choice with the fastest response. Contrast and colour shift once you move off the specified viewing direction, so it fits devices with a fixed operator position.

The UART/serial variant carries its own controller and command set, so your MCU sends text and commands instead of driving pixels. It cuts firmware work substantially.

Touch is optional on this module. We bond capacitive or resistive panels to the same glass, so you can start without touch and add it on a later revision.

Ordering and customisation

This model ships as a bare module by default. We commonly modify the FPC length and connector, change the backlight current, add a capacitive or resistive touch panel, or bond cover glass with your printing. Tell us the enclosure opening and the mounting method and we will confirm what can stay standard and what needs tooling.

How a customised order runs

  1. Send the requirement

    Size, resolution, interface, brightness, operating temperature, touch or no touch, and the annual quantity. A photo or drawing of the enclosure opening helps more than a long description.

  2. We confirm feasibility and quote

    You get a specific model number or a modification plan, with tooling cost and lead time stated separately from the unit price. Usually within one working day.

  3. Sample and approval

    Samples in 10-15 days from stock glass; custom glass or a new FPC adds tooling time. You approve dimensions, display quality and interface timing before mass production starts.

  4. Production and shipment

    10-20 days after specification freeze. Anti-static bag + foam tray + export carton, shipped DHL / FedEx / UPS express or by your forwarder. We keep the approved sample as the reference for every repeat order.

Questions about this model

What is the minimum order quantity for the Multiple TFT display customization?

Samples are 5 pcs. Production MOQ is 100 pcs for this catalogue model and 1,000 pcs when the FPC, backlight or glass is modified.

How long is the lead time?

10-20 days for production quantities, 10-15 days for samples from stock glass. Custom tooling is quoted separately and confirmed in writing first.

Can this module be customised?

Yes - FPC length and pin order, connector type, backlight brightness and colour temperature, cover glass, touch panel and bonding method. See what is changeable.

Do you provide a datasheet and drawing?

Yes. Email hyd@szjunxian.com with the model number and we send the full datasheet, 2D drawing and, where relevant, the initial sample report.

Which interface should I choose for my board?

It depends on your host processor and target resolution. Our interface selection guide compares MCU, SPI, RGB, LVDS and MIPI by pin count, resolution ceiling and MCU load.
