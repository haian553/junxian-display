Choosing between these 10.1" modules

Start from the interface

Your host processor decides this more than the panel does. An MCU without an LCD controller needs SPI or a smart module; an ARM board with a display controller can drive RGB; MIPI needs a DSI-capable SoC.

Then the glass mode

IPS keeps colour stable when the panel is read from an angle. TN costs less and is fine when the operator faces the screen. VA gives the deepest black.

Then brightness

Indoors 200-300 cd/m² is enough. Near windows or in workshops go to 350-450. Outdoors and in vehicles, 500 cd/m² and above.

Finally mechanics

Outline dimension, active area, FPC exit direction and connector type decide whether the module drops into your enclosure. All four are listed on each model page, and all four can be changed.

If two models look equivalent, the difference is usually the driver IC or the FPC layout. Send us your board and we will say which one integrates with fewer changes.
