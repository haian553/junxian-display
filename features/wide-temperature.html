When this is not the right spec

Wide-temperature glass and backlight are a real cost step, not a free upgrade - if the equipment lives in a heated enclosure or is never switched on cold, the standard -20 °C range covers it and there is no reason to pay for the wider rating.

Wide-Temperature modules - questions

What is the difference between operating range and cold start?

Operating range is how cold the display can run once it is on; cold start is whether it switches on reliably at that temperature in the first place. Equipment that has been off overnight in an unheated space needs the wide-temperature rating for cold start, not just for the average running temperature.

How much colder do wide-temperature modules go than standard?

Standard is -20 °C to +70 °C; the models on this page are rated -30 °C or colder, with most of our wide-temperature parts rated to -40 °C. Each model's exact figure is in the table above and on its own product page.

Do wide-temperature modules cost more?

Yes - the glass, sealant and backlight driving all change to hold performance at the colder rating. It is worth it only when the equipment genuinely sees that temperature; for a heated cabinet, standard range is the better value.
