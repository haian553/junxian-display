What we change on an existing panel

Most “custom display” projects are not custom glass at all. Everything in the first four boxes reuses existing glass and starts at 100 pcs with no tooling - which is the difference between a 10-20 days order and a tooling project.

FPC and connector

Length, pin order, exit direction, stiffener and connector type - the most requested change.

Backlight

Brightness, drive current and colour on monochrome parts. Decides power budget and lifetime.

Touch and cover glass

Capacitive or resistive, air or optical bonding, cover glass outline and printing.

Driver IC and PCBA

Controller selection within what the glass supports; on-board regulator or level shifting.

Glass itself

New size, shape or a custom segment layout. This is the one that needs tooling.

Frequently asked questions

What is your MOQ for LCD modules?

Samples start at 5 pcs. Standard catalogue items are 100 pcs, and fully custom modules (custom FPC, backlight, glass or bonding) are 1,000 pcs. If your first order is below MOQ, tell us the annual forecast - we usually accept a smaller pilot batch when a schedule follows.

How long does production take?

10-20 days after the specification and artwork are frozen. Samples from stock glass take 10-15 days. Fully custom segment glass or a new FPC layout adds tooling time, which we confirm in writing before you place the order.

Can you customise the FPC, backlight and driver IC?

Yes. We change FPC length, pin order and connector type, adjust backlight brightness and colour temperature, and select the driver IC to match your MCU. Cover glass, bonding type (air gap or optical bonding) and touch controller are also open. See capabilities for what is and is not changeable.

Do you support long-term supply for industrial products?

Industrial customers usually need 5-10 years of continuity. We hold the glass and driver IC combination for running projects and give written notice before any change. Read the EOL guide for how we handle end-of-life panels and drop-in replacements.

Which interfaces do you support?

MCU/8080, SPI, RGB (16/18/24-bit), MIPI DSI, LVDS and UART for smart modules. Choosing between them is the single most common question we get - the interface guide compares them by resolution ceiling, pin count and MCU load.

Are you a factory or a trading company?

A factory. 1,500 m² of production space in Bao'an, Shenzhen with 3 assembly lines, 19 machines and 52 staff (6 in R&D). Company registered in 2005. You are welcome to visit or ask for a live video walkthrough of the line running your order.

Need a quote or a datasheet?

Send us size, resolution, interface and annual quantity. Our engineers reply within one working day. Sample MOQ 5 pcs, lead time 10-20 days.
