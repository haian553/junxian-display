Get those right and the rest is mechanical.

How to choose

Which interface?

8-bit MCU with no LCD controller: SPI. ARM/Linux board: RGB. High resolution or few free pins: MIPI. Long cable or larger panel: LVDS.

Which glass mode?

Operator looks straight on and cost matters: TN. Panel read from an angle: IPS. Dark room, high contrast wanted: VA.

How much brightness?

Indoor instrument: 200-300 cd/m². Workshop or near a window: 350-450 cd/m². Outdoors or in a vehicle: 500 cd/m² and up.

Wide temperature?

If the enclosure is unheated or goes outdoors, specify -20 °C or lower - commercial panels stop at 0 °C.

Still unsure? The technical guides go through these decisions with numbers, or send us the application and we will shortlist two or three models.
