Capacitive and Resistive Touch Screen LCD Modules

22 models from 2.8" to 10.1", with RGB parallel, MIPI DSI and MCU / 8080 / 6800 parallel interfaces. Samples 5 pcs, lead time 10-20 days. A touch module is a TFT panel with a touch sensor bonded to it. What you are really buying is the bonding: air-gap bonding is cheaper and repairable, optical bonding removes the air layer so the screen stays readable in bright light and survives condensation. We do both in-house, which is why a changed cover glass or a different controller does not push the schedule out by weeks.

How to choose

Capacitive or resistive?

Capacitive for consumer-feel UIs and multi-touch; resistive when gloves, styluses or water are involved.

Air-gap or optical bonding?

Optical bonding for bright light, condensation risk and a premium feel; air gap when cost and repairability matter more.

Cover glass?

We bond your printed cover glass, including logo, icons and a fixed bezel area - decide the artwork before tooling.

Gloves or wet use?

Say so up front: it changes the sensor stack and controller tuning, and is expensive to fix later.

Still unsure? The technical guides go through these decisions with numbers, or send us the application and we will shortlist two or three models.
