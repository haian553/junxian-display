How to choose

Format

16x2 is the industry default; 20x4 when you need more context on screen at once.

Interface

6800 parallel is classic and cheapest; I2C or SPI saves pins on small MCUs.

Backlight colour

Yellow-green, blue with white characters, or white on black - a purely aesthetic decision with no cost difference.

Still unsure? The technical guides go through these decisions with numbers, or send us the application and we will shortlist two or three models.
