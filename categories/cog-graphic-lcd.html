Graphic LCD Modules

22 models from 1.5" to 5.7", with MCU / 8080 / 6800 parallel and SPI interfaces. Samples 5 pcs, lead time 10-20 days. Graphic LCD modules are monochrome dot-matrix displays for graphics and multi-line text. Most of ours are COG (chip-on-glass): the driver IC sits directly on the glass, giving a thin, low-cost module. They run for years on batteries and stay readable in daylight - the reason meters and portable instruments still specify them instead of colour.

How to choose

Resolution

128x64 covers most instrument UIs; 192x64 and 240x128 when you need more lines or a waveform.

Polarizer mode

Transflective for daylight readability, transmissive with backlight for dark environments.

Backlight

LED backlight is optional - leaving it out can multiply battery life on a meter.

Still unsure? The technical guides go through these decisions with numbers, or send us the application and we will shortlist two or three models.

Graphic LCD Modules - common questions

What is the MOQ for graphic lcd modules?

Samples 5 pcs, standard production 100 pcs, fully custom 1,000 pcs.

Can I get a datasheet before ordering?

Yes, on request. Email hyd@szjunxian.com with the model number, or list your parameters and we will propose models that fit.

Do you supply the panel only, or a complete assembly?

Both, by default a bare module. We also bond touch panels and cover glass, fit custom FPCs and connectors, and supply a driver board where the design needs one.

What if none of the listed models fits?

About 120 new models a year start exactly this way: we modify a close model or build to your drawing. Send the size, resolution and interface you need.
