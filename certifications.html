What we do not claim. A display module is a component, not a finished product. CE and FCC marks apply to your end device, not to a bare module - a supplier that puts a CE badge on a bare LCD is selling you something that will not help your declaration of conformity. What we can do is supply the material declarations and test data your own certification needs.

How to verify a Chinese supplier's certificates

Useful whoever you end up buying from:

  1. Ask for the certificate number and the issuing body, not a picture. Certificate numbers can be checked with the certification body directly. Ours is U24Q2GZ8029389R0S, issued by GICG (Guardian Independent Certification Ltd).
  2. Check the scope. A quality system certificate lists what activity and what site it covers. A certificate for a trading office does not cover a factory. Ours reads “manufacturing and sales of LCD screens” at the Bao'an site.
  3. Check the dates. Certificates expire and require surveillance audits. Ours runs 19 March 2024 to 18 March 2027. Note the first certification date too: ours is 19 March 2024, so we do not claim to have been certified for longer than we have been.
  4. Check the legal entity name matches the company that will invoice you. Ours is Shenzhen Junxian Electronic Technology Co., Ltd., credit code 91440300777163896P.
  5. Ask for the business licence and compare the registration date with the “established in” claim on the website. Ours says 2005-07-05, which is why this site says 2005 and not a rounder number.

We are happy to be checked this way, and we suggest you apply the same five steps to every quote you are comparing ours against. More on this in our ordering guide.
