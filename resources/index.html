RFQ parameter checklist

Send these six values and you get a model number, a unit price at your quantity, tooling cost stated separately if any, and a schedule - in one round instead of three.

Screen sizee.g. 4.3", or the enclosure opening dimensions
Resolutione.g. 800 x 480, or the detail level your UI needs
InterfaceMCU, SPI, RGB, LVDS, MIPI, UART - or tell us your processor
Brightness / environmentIndoor, workshop, outdoor, in-vehicle
TouchNone, capacitive or resistive; gloves or wet use?
QuantityFirst order and annual forecast

Segment LCD specification template

Resources - questions

Do you provide datasheets as downloadable PDFs?

Full PDF datasheets are issued with a quotation for the specific model - the drawings here are the mechanical outline for models with a full published specification, useful for a first fit check before you request the complete datasheet.

Can I get an editable version of the RFQ checklist?

The parameters on this page are the same six fields on our quote form - fill them in directly on the quote page, or copy this list into your own purchasing template and email it to us.

Do you provide driver IC initialisation code?

Yes, on request with the model number - ask and we supply the initialisation sequence verified for that specific module and glass batch. This is not yet published in bulk on this page; ask directly for now.

Need a quote or a datasheet?

Send us size, resolution, interface and annual quantity. Our engineers reply within one working day. Sample MOQ 5 pcs, lead time 10-20 days.
