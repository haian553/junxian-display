Interfaces

SPI LCD Display Modules

Four to five wires (clock, data, chip select, command/data, optional reset); the panel's own driver IC holds the frame buffer and the host only sends what has changed. It is the lowest pin count of any interface that still drives a colour TFT, which is why it dominates pin-starved and battery-powered designs.

When SPI is not the right choice

Not the right choice for full-screen video or fast animation at larger resolutions - the bus becomes the bottleneck well before the panel does. Above roughly QVGA, an MCU-parallel or RGB interface will move data faster for the same clock effort.

SPI - questions

How many published SPI models do you have right now?

19 on the site today, but SPI is a standard option across our small-panel TFT and COG lines - we develop roughly 120 new models a year, so ask for your exact size and resolution before assuming it is not available.

What frame rate can I actually get over SPI?

It depends on resolution, colour depth and SPI clock - use the calculator embedded in our SPI frame rate guide to work it out for your numbers rather than assuming a figure. As a rule, partial redraw (updating only what changed) gets you much further than a full-frame refresh at any clock speed.

Does SPI need a display controller on my MCU?

No - the panel's own driver IC holds the frame buffer, which is exactly why SPI works on MCUs that have no display hardware at all.
