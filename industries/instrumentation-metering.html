Instruments show the same few numbers for years, often on battery. We build custom TN, HTN and VATN segment glass, COG graphic modules from 128x64 to 240x128, and character modules from 8x2 to 20x4.

Three things that go wrong

Written from the enquiries we have to correct most often.

Using a graphic module where a segment glass belongs

If the display always shows the same fields, a custom segment glass is cheaper per unit, draws less current and needs almost no firmware. The trade is tooling: segment glass needs a mask and 1,000 pcs to be economic. Below that, a COG graphic module is the right compromise.

Forgetting the viewing direction at design time

Segment and STN glass has a preferred viewing angle - 6 o'clock and 12 o'clock builds are physically different parts. A panel-mounted meter read from above and a handheld read from below need opposite choices, and this is fixed in the glass, not in firmware.

Specifying a backlight you do not need

The backlight is usually the largest single load in a battery meter. Reflective glass needs none in daylight; a switched backlight for occasional night reading costs far less energy than a permanently lit transmissive panel.

What we can change - and what we cannot

Changed on a catalogue panel

FPC length, pin order and exit direction; connector type; backlight brightness and colour; touch panel and cover glass; driver IC selection within what the glass supports; PCBA and firmware on smart serial modules. These start at 100 pcs and do not need new glass tooling.

Needs new tooling

A new glass size or shape, a custom segment layout, a new touch sensor pattern. Tooling MOQ is 1,000 pcs and we quote the tooling cost separately so you can see what is one-off and what is per-piece.

What we will not claim

We do not quote a panel we cannot build to your temperature, brightness and lifetime figures at the same time. If one of the three has to give, we say which one before you order samples rather than after.

Instrumentation & Metering displays - questions

Can you make a custom segment LCD for our meter?

Yes - custom TN, HTN and VATN segment glass is one of our core lines. You send the layout (or a photo of the current display and a sketch), we return a drawing for approval, then tooling. Custom glass MOQ is 1,000 pcs; tooling cost and sample timing are quoted per layout.

What is the difference between TN, HTN and VATN?

TN is the cheapest and lowest contrast. HTN improves contrast and viewing angle at a small premium. VATN gives near-black background with bright characters and the widest viewing angle, and is what most modern instrument fronts use. The choice mostly follows how premium the front panel needs to look.

Do you have displays that work without a backlight?

Yes. Reflective and transflective segment and COG glass is readable in ambient light with no backlight at all, which is what makes multi-year battery life possible. Transflective parts add a backlight for dark conditions without losing daylight readability.

Which interface do your monochrome modules use?

6800/8080 parallel, SPI (including 4-wire SPI) and I²C, depending on the model. The interface of every published model is listed in the table on this page - pick the one your MCU already has free pins for.

What are the MOQ and lead time?

Catalogue COG and character modules start at 100 pcs with 10-20 days lead time. Custom segment glass needs tooling and 1,000 pcs. Samples of catalogue parts are 5 pcs.

Need a quote or a datasheet?

Send us size, resolution, interface and annual quantity. Our engineers reply within one working day. Sample MOQ 5 pcs, lead time 10-20 days.
