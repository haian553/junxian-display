Small LCD and OLED Modules for Handheld Terminals and POS Devices

A handheld device is a power budget with a screen attached. Between 2" and 5" the real decisions are how many pins the display costs you, how much current the backlight draws, and whether the screen survives being read outdoors. We build 2" to 5" IPS and TFT modules with SPI, MCU and RGB interfaces, plus OLED modules that need no backlight at all.

Three things that go wrong

Written from the enquiries we have to correct most often.

Budgeting the backlight from the typical figure

Datasheet brightness is quoted at a drive current you may not be able to sustain on battery. Decide the real duty cycle first: a 500 cd/m² panel run at 40 % brightness behaves nothing like the datasheet line, and that is usually the right way to run it.

Using a parallel interface on a pin-starved MCU

An 8-bit parallel panel costs you a dozen pins and the PCB routing that goes with them. Below about 3.5", SPI is fast enough for most UIs and frees the pins for the scanner, radio and card reader.

Treating OLED as a drop-in for LCD

OLED gives perfect black, needs no backlight and is thin - but a static UI element left on screen for months will burn in, and outdoor brightness is limited. Use it for short sessions and dark UIs; use IPS for a screen that is on all shift.

What we can change - and what we cannot

Changed on a catalogue panel

FPC length, pin order and exit direction; connector type; backlight brightness and colour; touch panel and cover glass; driver IC selection within what the glass supports; PCBA and firmware on smart serial modules. These start at 100 pcs and do not need new glass tooling.

Needs new tooling

A new glass size or shape, a custom segment layout, a new touch sensor pattern. Tooling MOQ is 1,000 pcs and we quote the tooling cost separately so you can see what is one-off and what is per-piece.

What we will not claim

We do not quote a panel we cannot build to your temperature, brightness and lifetime figures at the same time. If one of the three has to give, we say which one before you order samples rather than after.

Handheld Terminals & POS displays - questions

What size displays do you make for handheld devices?

2" to 5" is the usual band and the table on this page lists what is published. Smaller OLED modules from 0.49" go into status displays and accessories; larger parts move into desktop POS.

SPI or parallel interface for a battery device?

SPI in almost every case below 3.5": fewer pins, simpler routing, less switching noise, and enough bandwidth for a UI that is not full-motion video. Move to 8-bit MCU or RGB when you need fast full-screen redraws.

Can you bond a custom cover glass to the touch panel?

Yes - cover glass outline, thickness, printing and the bonding method are specified per project. Optical bonding gives better contrast and impact resistance than air bonding at a higher cost; we quote both so the trade is yours.

Are your small modules readable in sunlight?

Partly - that is a brightness and polariser question. Parts at 350 to 500 cd/m² are usable in shade and indirect daylight. Direct sun on a 5" panel needs a transflective or high-brightness build, which we quote separately rather than implying that a standard part will do it.

What is the MOQ for a small custom module?

100 pcs for a catalogue model with minor changes (FPC, connector, backlight), 1,000 pcs where new glass or a new touch sensor is tooled. Samples 5 pcs, 10-15 days.
