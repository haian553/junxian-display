BMS Protocol Serial LCD Modules for Energy Storage and EV Charging

Battery and charging equipment has a display problem that is really a firmware problem: the BMS already knows the state of charge, and nobody wants to write a graphics stack to show it. Our smart serial modules take UART commands and render the screen themselves - including parts built for BMS protocols - so the host sends values, not pixels. Outdoor chargers additionally need brightness and a cold-start range, both listed per model below.

Three things that go wrong

Written from the enquiries we have to correct most often.

Writing a display stack you did not need

The most common avoidable cost here is firmware. A smart serial module with an on-board controller turns the display into a handful of UART commands, which is weeks of engineering that never has to happen. It costs more per unit than a dumb panel and is usually still cheaper overall below a few thousand units.

Specifying brightness for the enclosure, not the sun

An indoor cabinet is happy at 250 cd/m². A charging post facing south is not. Above roughly 400 cd/m² the backlight adds heat inside a sealed enclosure that is already warm, so brightness, enclosure venting and panel lifetime have to be decided together.

Confusing operating range with cold start

A panel rated to -20 °C will run at -20 °C once warm, but a charger that has been off overnight has to start there. Where the equipment is unheated and outdoors, specify the wide-temperature part and, if the UI must be readable immediately, a heater film - we will tell you which of the two your case actually needs.

What we can change - and what we cannot

Changed on a catalogue panel

FPC length, pin order and exit direction; connector type; backlight brightness and colour; touch panel and cover glass; driver IC selection within what the glass supports; PCBA and firmware on smart serial modules. These start at 100 pcs and do not need new glass tooling.

Needs new tooling

A new glass size or shape, a custom segment layout, a new touch sensor pattern. Tooling MOQ is 1,000 pcs and we quote the tooling cost separately so you can see what is one-off and what is per-piece.

What we will not claim

We do not quote a panel we cannot build to your temperature, brightness and lifetime figures at the same time. If one of the three has to give, we say which one before you order samples rather than after.

Energy Storage, BMS & EV Charging displays - questions

Do you have display modules that speak BMS protocols?

Yes - that is what our smart serial line is built around. The module carries its own controller and takes UART commands, so the BMS sends values and the screen renders them. Send us the protocol document or the command list you need to support and we will confirm compatibility before you order samples.

What is a smart serial LCD module?

A display with an on-board controller and a command set over UART. Instead of driving pixels, the host sends instructions like 'show this value in this field'. It removes the graphics work from your firmware and lets a small MCU drive a colour screen it could not otherwise handle.

How bright does an outdoor EV charger display need to be?

Direct sun needs 500 cd/m² or a transflective build to stay readable; shaded or canopy-mounted posts are usually fine at 350 to 450 cd/m². Higher brightness means more backlight heat inside a sealed enclosure, so it is a thermal decision as much as an optical one.

Do you have wide-temperature panels for outdoor equipment?

Yes, -40 °C to +70 °C operating with storage to +85 °C on wide-temperature parts, against -20 °C to +70 °C as standard. The rating of each published model is in the table on this page.
