Round and Smart Serial LCD Modules for Automotive Aftermarket Instrument Clusters

A retrofit gauge or an EV conversion dashboard has to survive a cabin that swings from a cold overnight start to a hot closed car in summer, fit a round or small cutout that was never designed for electronics, and talk to a controller over a couple of wires rather than a full display bus. We build round panels from 1.28" to 3.4", OLED modules for compact clusters, and smart serial modules that render a screen from simple commands - plus panels rated to switch on at -30 °C or colder.

Three things that go wrong

Written from the enquiries we have to correct most often.

Assuming a round panel is a drop-in gauge replacement

A round display is a display, not a gauge with a needle motor's mounting pattern. Bezel diameter, cutout depth and how the FPC exits the back of the panel all have to match the pod or dash cutout before glass is ordered - send the mechanical drawing, not just the diagonal size.

Specifying standard temperature for a vehicle that sits outside

Standard-range panels are rated to -20 °C. A vehicle parked outside overnight in a cold climate sees colder than that at the moment it is switched on - that is a cold-start question, not an average-temperature one. Use the wide-temperature parts, not the standard range, for anything mounted outside a heated cabin.

Writing a graphics driver for a single gauge value

If the display only ever shows a small set of values - speed, state of charge, a warning icon - a smart serial module removes the graphics work entirely. Writing a driver for a bare panel makes sense when the UI is complex; for a single-purpose gauge it is usually wasted engineering time.

What we can change - and what we cannot

Changed on a catalogue panel

FPC length, pin order and exit direction; connector type; backlight brightness and colour; touch panel and cover glass; driver IC selection within what the glass supports; PCBA and firmware on smart serial modules. These start at 100 pcs and do not need new glass tooling.

Needs new tooling

A new glass size or shape, a custom segment layout, a new touch sensor pattern. Tooling MOQ is 1,000 pcs and we quote the tooling cost separately so you can see what is one-off and what is per-piece.

What we will not claim

We do not quote a panel we cannot build to your temperature, brightness and lifetime figures at the same time. If one of the three has to give, we say which one before you order samples rather than after.

Automotive Aftermarket & EV Instrument Clusters displays - questions

Do you supply displays for automotive OEM production?

No - we are not IATF 16949 certified and do not claim automotive-grade qualification. What we build well is aftermarket, retrofit and EV-conversion instrument displays: round and small-format panels and smart serial modules for gauge clusters, dashboards and handlebar pods, at volumes from 5 pcs samples upward.

Can a round display survive being mounted on a dashboard or handlebar?

The panel is rated the same as any of our modules; what changes for a vehicle mount is the FPC routing, connector choice and how the glass is bonded into your bracket - tell us the mounting method and we will flag anything that needs a different connector or a stiffer FPC.

What operating temperature do I need for an outdoor vehicle display?

If the vehicle is stored outside in a cold climate, specify our wide-temperature parts (-30 °C or colder) rather than the -20 °C standard range - the display has to switch on cold, not just tolerate cold once it is already running.

Do your smart serial modules support a simple gauge protocol?

Yes - they take UART commands and render the screen themselves, so a small MCU can drive a colour gauge display by sending values rather than pixels. Send us the value set you need to display and we will confirm the command list.
