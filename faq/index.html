Ordering and MOQ

What is your minimum order quantity?

5 pcs for samples, 100 pcs for catalogue models, and 1,000 pcs where new tooling is needed - for example a new segment layout, glass size or touch sensor pattern. The same three figures are stated on every product page.

What is your lead time?

10-20 days for production, 10-15 days for samples. Tooling projects add the tooling cycle on top - see our custom development guide for the stage-by-stage timing.

Can I order below your standard MOQ?

Ask - catalogue models sometimes have room below 100 pcs depending on the specific part and current production scheduling. Custom tooling projects are firmer on 1,000 pcs because that reflects the real cost of setting up the run.

What payment methods do you accept?

T/T, L/C, PayPal.

What are your Incoterms?

EXW, FOB Shenzhen, CIF. If your project needs delivered-duty-paid terms, tell us before quotation - see our HS code and duties guide for what that changes.

How do I get a firm quotation quickly?

Send screen size, resolution, interface, touch requirement, brightness, operating temperature and annual quantity in one message. Those seven values are usually enough for a specific quotation in one round instead of three.

Do you charge for samples?

Sample pricing depends on whether the part is a catalogue model or needs new tooling - ask with your specification and we will state the sample cost alongside the quotation.

Can I get a formal quotation in writing for procurement approval?

Yes - every quotation is issued in writing with the model number, unit price at your quantity, tooling cost stated separately where applicable, and a schedule.

Customisation

What can you change on an existing panel without new tooling?

FPC length and exit direction, connector type, backlight brightness and colour temperature, cover glass, touch panel and bonding method, and driver IC selection within what the glass supports. These start at 100 pcs and do not need new glass tooling.

What requires new tooling?

A new glass size or shape, a custom segment layout, or a new touch sensor pattern. Tooling MOQ is 1,000 pcs, and the tooling cost is quoted separately from the per-piece price so you can see what is one-off.

Can you modify a competitor's module to match our existing design?

Often, if you can supply the drawing, the sample, or both. We will name candidates and state honestly where each one differs before you commit to matching one exactly.

Do you develop fully custom modules from a drawing?

Yes - that is the core of our business. See our custom development guide for the six-stage process and what is billed as NRE versus per-piece.

Can I get capacitive touch added to a panel that currently has none?

Yes - touch bonding is a separate step from the base panel. Tell us capacitive or resistive and whether you need air-gap or optical bonding.

Can you add anti-glare or anti-reflective coating to the cover glass?

Yes - surface treatment is changed independently of the panel underneath, on new orders and on modifications to an existing design.

Can you match our brand colour on the bezel or add our logo?

Yes - send a Pantone reference or artwork file and we will confirm the closest achievable match and any minimum feature size before tooling.

Do you supply datasheets and 2D drawings for customised parts?

Yes, sent with the quotation and confirmed again at sample approval, so what you design against matches what ships.

Technical and specifications

Which interface should I use?

Check your processor's datasheet for a display or LCD controller block. If it has one, use what it drives - RGB, LVDS or MIPI DSI. If it does not, choose between SPI, MCU-parallel, I2C or a UART smart module depending on pin budget and update speed. Our interface guide walks through the full decision.

How bright does my display need to be?

200-300 cd/m² indoors, 350-450 cd/m² near windows or under workshop lighting, 500 cd/m² or higher - or a transflective build - for direct outdoor sun. See our brightness guide for the reasoning and the brightness selector tool.

What operating temperature range do you support?

-20 °C to +70 °C as standard, with wide-temperature parts rated to -40 °C or colder for equipment that switches on cold. See our wide-temperature guide for the difference between operating range and cold start.

What does your backlight lifetime figure actually mean?

40,000 hours is an L50 figure - the time to half brightness under continuous drive at rated current and 25 °C, not a failure point or a warranty term. See our backlight lifetime guide for what shortens and extends it in real use.

Capacitive or resistive touch - which do I need?

Capacitive for a consumer-feel, multi-touch UI operated bare-fingered; resistive when gloves, a stylus, or a wet or dusty screen are part of the use case. Several of our models leave the choice selectable at order time.

TN, IPS, VA or STN - which glass mode should I choose?

It depends on viewing angle and contrast requirements at your operating temperature - see our glass mode comparison for the full trade-off.

What is the difference between a smart serial module and a bare panel?

A smart serial (UART) module has its own controller and takes commands instead of pixels, removing the graphics stack from your firmware at the cost of a higher unit price and a bounded command set. See our smart serial versus bare TFT guide for the full comparison.

Do you provide initialisation code for your driver ICs?

Yes - ask with the model number and we supply the initialisation sequence verified for that specific module and glass batch, including reset timing and VCOM/gamma values.

What happens if my display is discontinued or end-of-life elsewhere?

Send the model, photos and dimensions and we will identify an equivalent or quote a module version. See our end-of-life guide for how to define equivalence before you requalify.

Quality and certification

Are you a factory or a trading company?

A manufacturer with in-house production and in-house sampling. Our factory page publishes dated photographs of the plant floor, and our certifications page publishes certificate numbers you can verify independently - see our guide on verifying a Shenzhen factory for how to check both.

What quality certifications do you hold?

ISO 9001:2015, certificate no. U24Q2GZ8029389R0S, issued by GICG (Guardian Independent Certification Ltd), verifiable on the CNCA public register. Details and the scan are on our certifications page.

Are your products RoHS compliant?

A submitted sample was tested and passed against all ten restricted substances under RoHS 2011/65/EU as amended by (EU) 2015/863, report no. A2260556881101. This is a sample verification report, not a blanket certification of every model - ask for the full report or a material declaration for your specific part.

What quality checks does a module go through before it ships?

Incoming inspection, in-process checks during assembly, ageing tests and outgoing inspection before packing - see our quality control page for what is checked at each stage.

Can I get a sample approved before production starts?

Yes - the approved sample is the reference for every repeat order. You approve dimensions, display quality and interface timing before mass production begins.

What happens if a batch has a defect?

Tell us the model, batch and symptom - we confirm init settings and process conditions per glass batch on our side, and would rather investigate at one sample than at one thousand units.

Shipping and logistics

How do you ship orders?

DHL / FedEx / UPS express, air freight, sea freight, depending on order size and urgency.

How are modules packed?

Anti-static bag + foam tray + export carton. Desiccant is available on request for sea freight or humid destinations.

Who is responsible for import duties?

Under our standard terms (EXW, FOB Shenzhen, CIF), import clearance and duties in your country are the buyer's responsibility. See our HS code and duties guide for how classification works and what documents we provide.

What documents do you provide for customs clearance?

Commercial invoice, packing list and country of origin as standard; a certificate of origin can be arranged on request. Origin on all shipments is Shenzhen, China.

Can you ship samples by express courier?

Yes - DHL, FedEx or UPS express is the usual route for samples, with sea or air freight for production quantities depending on order size.

Do you offer delivered-duty-paid (DDP) shipping?

Not as a standard term - ask before quotation if your project needs DDP so it can be arranged and priced into the commercial terms upfront.

Need a quote or a datasheet?

Send us size, resolution, interface and annual quantity. Our engineers reply within one working day. Sample MOQ 5 pcs, lead time 10-20 days.
